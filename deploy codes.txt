Why TCP instead of wss
Over a bare IP there's no TLS certificate, and the publisher forces TLS whenever transport: websockets — so ws:// on port 80 would fail the handshake. Direct MQTT TCP on 1883 is the clean no-TLS path. (You already opened 1883 in the firewall step.)
